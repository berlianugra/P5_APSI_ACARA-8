About

PT. Yamatogomu Indonesia is a manufacturer of Rubber Parts for automotive with long experience in material and molding. Also, we promise to meet our customer requirements of Quality, Cost, and Delivery date. Please consider us as a good partner to help you achieve your requirements or target.

Manufacturing Process

Our manufacturing process combines precision, consistency, and quality control at every stage to produce reliable rubber compounds and automotive components. From raw material preparation to final product inspection and delivery, each process is carefully carried out to meet customer specifications and maintain product quality.

Material Preparation

Raw Material Receiving & Inspection

Raw materials are received from suppliers and undergo an initial inspection to ensure their condition and specifications meet the company's requirements. This inspection helps prevent unsuitable materials from entering the production process.

Raw Material Storage (WHRM)

Materials that pass the initial inspection are stored in the Warehouse Raw Material (WHRM). They are organized and kept in designated storage areas before being used in the manufacturing process.

Material Weighing

Various materials, including chemicals, carbon, polymer, and oil, are weighed according to the Working Plan Sheet (WPS). Accurate weighing is essential to maintain the correct formulation and consistency of the rubber compound.

Production Process

Kneader Mixing

The prepared materials are mixed using a kneader machine to combine the different ingredients into a rubber compound. This process helps achieve the required material composition and characteristics based on the production specifications.

Open Rolling & Cutting

The rubber compound is further processed through an open rolling machine to improve material uniformity and ensure proper mixing. After rolling, the compound is cut into specific forms, such as sheets or ribbons, for the next production stage.

Cooling Down

After the mixing and rolling processes, the rubber compound undergoes a cooling process to reduce its temperature. This step prepares the compound for quality inspection and subsequent handling.

Compound Storage

Compounds that have passed quality inspection are stored in a designated storage area. This semi-finished material is kept until it is required for the production of specific automotive components.

Molding & Vulcanization

The rubber compound is shaped into automotive components through a molding process using injection press or compression press machines. The material then undergoes vulcanization to develop the required physical properties and final product shape.

Quality Control & Packaging

Compound Quality Inspection

The rubber compound undergoes quality inspection after the mixing and cooling processes. This inspection ensures that the material meets the required quality standards before being approved for further production.

Final Product Inspection

Finished automotive components are inspected to identify defects and ensure they meet the required product specifications. Products that do not meet the quality standards are identified as non-conforming products.

Product Weighing & Packing

Products that pass the final inspection are weighed according to customer requirements. They are then packed using the appropriate packaging method to maintain product condition and facilitate handling.

Finished Goods Storage (WHFG)

Packed products are transferred to the Warehouse Finished Goods (WHFG) for temporary storage. The products are organized and prepared for distribution according to customer orders and delivery schedules.

Shipping

The finished products are prepared for shipment and delivered to customers based on the agreed delivery requirements. This stage marks the completion of the manufacturing and distribution process.
